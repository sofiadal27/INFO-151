Associations

  • Institue of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE)

    IEEE is a professional association and global community for electrical engineering, electronics engineering, and other related disciplines. It is the world's largest technical professional organization dedicated to advancing technology for the benefit of humanity.

  • Society of Women Engineers (SWE)

  • Engineers Without Borders (EWB)

    EWB is a nonprofit organization that partners with communities around the world to design and implement sustainable engineering projects that improve quality of life. It is a way to apply technical skills in real-world, socially impactful ways.
